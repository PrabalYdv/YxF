Young & Freaks is a community built around fitness, art, creativity, healing and human connection — bringing together like-minded people from all across Patiala.

You don't have to fit in here.

You just have to be yourself.

From runs and movement to creative sessions, conversations, shared experiences and spaces to simply breathe — Young & Freaks is about becoming healthier while staying curious, expressive, and a little freaky.
